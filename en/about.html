Company Profile

Shanghai Zi'Ai Industrial Co., Ltd. was established in July 2014 with a registered capital of RMB 1,000,000.

We are a technology services and product sales company focused on industrial and technology sectors. Our core team consists of experienced senior engineers with deep expertise in IT hardware/software and industrial products.

Our main products include: Hardware Standard Parts, Sealing Components, Fasteners, Industrial Nameplates, Tools & Measuring Instruments, Electronics, Computers & Peripherals, Video/Audio/Conference Systems, Safety Equipment, Office Supplies, Shelving & Display Racks, and more. Our products are widely used in industrial machinery, automotive manufacturing, electronics, construction, petrochemical, food & pharmaceutical, energy and power industries.

Throughout our development, we have always adhered to the principles of "Customer First, Quality First." We have established a comprehensive supply chain management system to ensure stable product quality, timely delivery, and excellent service. Our customers are located across China and have gradually expanded to overseas markets including the USA, Europe, Southeast Asia, Middle East, and Africa.

Our Vision

To become a leading one-stop industrial products supplier in China, providing quality products and professional technical services to global customers.
