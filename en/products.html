Sealing Components

Product Overview

O-rings, sealing gaskets, mechanical seals, oil seals, and more. Widely used in chemical, oil & gas, pharmaceutical, food processing, water treatment, automotive, aerospace industries. Long-term partnerships with leading domestic and international brands ensure reliable quality.

Product Series
  • O-Rings：NBR, Viton (FKM), EPDM, silicone, fluorosilicone. Sizes from ID 1mm to 500mm. Custom shapes and materials available
  • Gaskets：Asbestos gaskets, non-asbestos gaskets, PTFE gaskets, spiral wound gaskets. Temperature range: -200°C to +800°C
  • Mechanical Seals：Single seal, double seal, reactor agitator seals. Shaft sizes 10mm~200mm, suitable for pumps, compressors, reactors
  • Oil Seals：TC, SC, VC type hub seals. Materials: NBR, FKM, polyurethane. Max temperature: 300°C
  • Other Seals：Dust boots, guide rings, piston seals, valve stem seals. Special materials on request
Applications

Chemical | Oil & Gas | Pharmaceutical | Food Processing | Water Treatment | Automotive | Aerospace | Power & Energy
